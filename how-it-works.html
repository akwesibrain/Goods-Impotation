MW–01

You tell us what you need

Fill in the request form with a description of the item — the more detail, the better (photos, links, size, color, quantity). If you're not sure how to describe it, just send what you know; we'll ask follow-up questions on WhatsApp.

What helps most

A photo or a link to the exact item (from 1688, Alibaba, Instagram, anywhere) gets you the most accurate quote, fastest.

Typical turnaround

Most quotes come back within 24 hours. Items from Turkey with an active supplier relationship can be even faster.

MW–02

We source it and quote you in GH₵

We check current pricing directly from suppliers in China or Turkey, factor in shipping and clearing, and send you one clear price in Ghana Cedis on WhatsApp — no hidden costs added later.

MW–03

You confirm, we purchase and ship

Once you approve the quote and terms discussed on WhatsApp, we place the order with the supplier and arrange shipping to Ghana. We'll keep you updated on progress.

Payment

Payment terms are agreed directly with you on WhatsApp before we proceed — we'll walk you through it.

Delivery

We deliver to your location across Ghana once your item clears — no need to travel to pick it up yourself.

MW–04

It arrives at your door

Your item clears customs and gets delivered to you. That's it — from a WhatsApp message to a package in your hands.
