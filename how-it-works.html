You tell us what you need

Fill in the request form with a description of the item — the more detail, the better (photos, links, size, color, quantity). If you're not sure how to describe it, just send what you know; we'll ask follow-up questions on chat.

What helps most

A photo or a link to the exact item (from Alibaba, Instagram, anywhere) gets you the most accurate quote, fastest.

Typical turnaround

Most quotes come back within 24 hours. Items from Turkey with an active supplier relationship can be even faster.
